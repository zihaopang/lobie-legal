Support

Lobie / 小額葉 · Daily Focus Drills

Get in touch

Email softnjer@yeah.net. One person reads it; expect a reply within a few days.

Please say which iPhone and which iOS version you are on — it saves a round trip.

I paid, but Pro is not showing

Settings → Restore Purchases. Make sure you are signed in with the same Apple Account that made the purchase. Family Sharing is not enabled for these purchases.

How do I cancel a subscription?

Subscriptions are billed by Apple, not by us, so they are cancelled in iOS: Settings → your name → Subscriptions → Lobie. The app's Settings screen has a shortcut to the same place. Cancelling stops the next renewal; the current period runs out as paid.

Refunds are handled by Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com. We cannot issue them.

The lifetime purchase

One payment, no renewal. It unlocks the same things the subscription does and never expires. If you already subscribed and then bought lifetime, cancel the subscription — it will not cancel itself.

Someone's nickname is offensive

Press and hold their row on the ranking screen → Report this name. Pick a reason. They disappear from your ranking immediately and the report reaches us. You can undo every block in Settings → Unblock everyone.

Changing my own nickname

Settings → Name on the board. Everyone in your group can see it, so do not use your real name. Obvious slurs are rejected at entry.

Leaving the ranking

Settings → turn off This week's ranking. The app stops uploading. If you also want the entries already on the leaderboard deleted, email us with the nickname you used and we will remove them.

Does my progress move to a new phone?

No. Your results, levels, streak and personal bests are stored on the device itself, not in iCloud. A new phone starts fresh. Purchases do carry over — use Restore Purchases.

Pro users can export everything to CSV first: Settings → Export my records.

Deleting my data

Deleting the app deletes everything it stored on the device. Settings → Delete all records does the same without removing the app. For ranking entries stored in iCloud, email us.

Will this make me smarter?

We do not claim that, and we will not. The evidence that this kind of training transfers to everyday attention or work performance is weak. What the app does is give you a short daily session and an honest record of how you did on it.
